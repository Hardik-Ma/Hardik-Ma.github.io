# NFCCU Operations & Amine Scrubber Simulation HPCL Mumbai Refinery

**Industrial Training Internship | July – August 2022**

![HPCL FCC GCU]({{ '/assets/img/projects/HPCL_FCC_GCU.jpg' | relative_url }})

## Overview

Summer internship at HPCL Mumbai Refinery (7.5 MMTPA capacity) focused on the **New Fluidized Catalytic Cracking Unit (NFCCU)** — a 1.456 MMTPA unit commissioned in 2011 using UOP licensed reactor-regenerator technology and BELCO flue gas scrubbing.

## What I Worked On

### NFCCU Process Study
Gained hands-on understanding of the full NFCCU process chain:

- **Reactor-Regenerator system** — UOP high-efficiency regenerator with combustor riser design, catalyst cooler, and recirculating catalyst standpipe. Reactor operates at ~527 °C / 1.62 kg/cm², regenerator at ~723 °C / 2.14 kg/cm² with a cat-to-oil ratio of 7.14.
- **Feed section** — Mixed feed (VGO, vacuum resid, heavy diesel, lube extracts, DAO) preheated through heat exchangers and furnace before injection via OPTIMIX feed nozzles.
- **Main Fractionator & Gas Concentration Unit (GCU)** — Separation of cracked vapors into fuel gas, LPG, light/heavy cracked naphtha, LCO, HCO, and resid.
- **Flue Gas Scrubbing Unit (FGSU)** — SOx and catalyst fines removal from regenerator flue gas before atmospheric discharge.

### Aspen Simulation — Amine Scrubber (GCU)

- Built a steady-state simulation of the **amine scrubber column** in the Gas Concentration Unit using **Aspen Plus / Aspen HYSYS**.
- The amine scrubber removes H₂S from the fuel gas stream before it enters the refinery fuel gas network.
- **Validated the simulation** against actual plant operating data and process parameters from the NFCCU amine treating section.
- Compared key outputs (H₂S removal efficiency, lean/rich amine loading, column temperature profile) with field measurements.
